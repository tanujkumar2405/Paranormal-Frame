About AERM

AERM (Anomalous Evidence Research & Media) is a paranormal investigation and research organization dedicated to evidence-based inquiry, systematic documentation, and public education regarding unexplained phenomena in India. We operate with scientific methodology, maintain ethical investigation practices, and promote critical thinking about paranormal claims.

AERM was founded by Tanuj Kumar, a B.Tech Computer Science Engineering graduate with specialization in Cybersecurity. His vision is to promote evidence-based paranormal investigation and responsible research practices in India. Learn more about him on our Founder page.

AERM is based in India and conducts investigations across Indian heritage sites including forts, palaces, temples, and historically significant locations. Our operations span multiple states in India with focus on locations having documented paranormal narratives and historical significance.

AERM (Anomalous Evidence Research & Media) is the parent organization focused on paranormal investigation and research. Paranormal Frame is the public-facing documentary and research initiative of AERM dedicated to transparency and education in paranormal investigation. Both maintain the same standards of scientific rigor and ethical practice.

Paranormal Investigation

Paranormal investigation is the systematic study and documentation of phenomena reported to be unexplained or beyond conventional scientific understanding. Professional paranormal investigators use scientific methodology, calibrated equipment, rigorous documentation, and critical analysis to examine reported paranormal events while maintaining objectivity and considering alternative explanations.

AERM uses a structured, multi-phase approach: (1) Historical research and location analysis, (2) Environmental baseline measurement, (3) Multi-phase investigation with calibrated instruments, (4) Multi-observer documentation protocols, (5) Data analysis and alternative hypothesis evaluation, (6) Comprehensive reporting. Every investigation prioritizes scientific rigor and transparent documentation.

AERM uses calibrated scientific instruments including EMF meters (detecting electromagnetic field variations), thermal imaging cameras (detecting temperature anomalies), audio recording equipment (capturing audio phenomena), environmental sensors (measuring environmental conditions), and data logging devices. All equipment undergoes regular calibration and is selected based on scientific reliability.

AERM investigates locations with documented paranormal reports or historical significance. While many of these are traditionally considered "haunted," we approach all investigations with evidence-based methodology rather than assumptions. We examine reported phenomena systematically to identify causes-which may be environmental, psychological, architectural, or remain unexplained after analysis.

Research & Methodology

AERM maintains rigorous scientific standards including documented methodology, calibrated instrumentation, multi-observer protocols, transparent data collection, and critical analysis. While paranormal research exists at boundaries of conventional academia, we maintain standards comparable to scientific research including peer review processes, reproducibility documentation, and publication of findings with full methodology transparency.

You can explore AERM research on ResearchGate, including the study "Environmental Factors Misidentified as Paranormal Activity: A Scientific Review of Physical, Chemical, Biological, Environmental, and Psychological Influences on Reported Hauntings." This work highlights how environmental and perceptual factors can be mistaken for paranormal events and reinforces our evidence-based investigation approach.

AERM follows evidence-based methodology: (1) Establish baseline measurements of normal conditions, (2) Systematically document observations and measurements, (3) Examine environmental, psychological, and physical explanations, (4) Consider all alternative hypotheses, (5) Report findings with full transparency about what was observed and what remains unexplained. We never assume paranormal causation without eliminating conventional explanations first.

We document and preserve all evidence transparently, noting every investigation method used, hypothesis tested, and alternative explanation considered. Unexplained phenomena are reported with full context about investigation conditions, equipment reliability, and analytical procedures. We acknowledge the limits of current understanding while maintaining intellectual honesty and avoiding unfounded claims about causation.

AERM investigations are funded through a combination of documentary initiatives, research programs, educational services, and community support. We maintain independence from external influence on research direction or findings interpretation. All investigation location and case selection is based on research merit and accessibility, never on financial considerations.

Research Concepts

Foundational concepts that explain how AERM evaluates evidence, documents investigations, and builds knowledge over time.

This mini research guide explains the core ideas behind our investigative work, including PFIRS, evidence classification, context analysis, and knowledge building from repeated case study research.

What is PFIRS?

PFIRS stands for Paranormal Forensic Investigation Reporting System. It is AERM's standard framework for documenting case evidence, investigation procedures, data sources, and analysis conclusions so every report is repeatable, transparent, and suitable for review.

What types of evidence does AERM classify?

AERM classifies evidence into audio evidence, environmental measurements, thermal and visual anomalies, eyewitness testimony, and documentary/archival data. Each type is recorded with context, instrumentation details, and confidence notes to create a complete knowledge record.

How does AERM build investigative knowledge?

AERM builds knowledge through repeated field observations, comparative case analysis, technique refinement, and publication of lessons learned. We treat each investigation as part of a larger research corpus, using documented findings to improve methods and identify patterns.

Why is context important in paranormal investigation?

Context is essential because reported phenomena can arise from environmental conditions, architectural features, cultural narratives, or psychological states. AERM always records the site context, historical background, and witness conditions before interpreting any data.

General Questions

AERM operates from an investigative perspective rather than a belief perspective. We investigate reported phenomena systematically and scientifically. Our investigations honor cultural heritage while maintaining scientific objectivity in evidence documentation and analysis.
