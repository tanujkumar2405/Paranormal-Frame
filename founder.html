Founder Introduction

Tanuj Kumar is the Founder and Director of AERM (Anomalous Evidence Research & Media), an independent paranormal investigation and research organization dedicated to evidence-based inquiry, systematic documentation, and public education regarding unexplained experiences and anomalous phenomena in India.

With a foundation in advanced technology and cybersecurity, Tanuj brings analytical rigor and technical expertise to paranormal research. His approach prioritizes scientific methodology, transparent documentation, critical thinking, and ethical investigation practices, establishing AERM as a respected resource for responsible paranormal research in India.

Tanuj Kumar — Founder & Director

As founder of AERM, Tanuj Kumar leads evidence-driven paranormal investigations across India. Combining technical expertise in cybersecurity with rigorous field methodology, he focuses on systematic data collection, multi-sensor documentation, and careful analysis to distinguish natural and environmental factors from genuine anomalous reports.

His investigations prioritise transparency, cultural respect, and reproducibility: calibrated instrument deployments, synchronized multi-observer recordings, and comprehensive contextual research. Tanuj publishes case reports and collaborates with interdisciplinary researchers to raise the standard of paranormal research in India.

The disciplined, systematic approach required in cybersecurity directly translates to meticulous investigation methodology and secure evidence preservation in paranormal research.

Paranormal Investigation Journey

Tanuj's fascination with unexplained phenomena and paranormal investigation began early, driven by curiosity about the scientific principles underlying reports of anomalous experiences. Rather than approaching paranormal claims with credulity, he developed a systematic, evidence-driven methodology grounded in scientific observation, environmental analysis, and critical evaluation.

Over years of dedicated research, Tanuj has investigated India's most historically significant and culturally important locations-forts, palaces, temples, and heritage sites-conducting rigorous fieldwork, equipment calibration, and multi-observer documentation. Each investigation contributes to a growing body of research on paranormal phenomena in India's unique geographical and cultural context.

His commitment to responsible investigation practices, ethical treatment of cultural sites, and transparent reporting has established him as a credible voice in paranormal research, respected for maintaining scientific standards in a field often subject to sensationalism.

Vision for AERM

Establish Credible Research Standards

Create internationally recognized standards for paranormal research in India, demonstrating that rigorous scientific methodology and paranormal investigation are compatible disciplines. AERM aims to elevate paranormal research above sensationalism toward serious academic and public discourse.

Advance Public Understanding

Educate the public about responsible paranormal investigation, critical thinking, and evidence-based evaluation of unusual claims. Through workshops, publications, and media collaborations, promote informed discussion about unexplained phenomena.

Preserve India's Paranormal Heritage

Document and preserve paranormal narratives associated with India's historical and cultural locations, ensuring that India's unique supernatural traditions are studied and respected through systematic, ethical investigation.

Foster Multidisciplinary Collaboration

Build partnerships with researchers across psychology, physics, history, and cultural studies to bring diverse expertise to paranormal phenomena. Encourage cross-disciplinary dialogue and collaborative investigation of anomalous experiences.
