Research & Credibility

Transparent Methodology & Scientific Standards

Paranormal Frame operates under institutional standards of documentation, transparency, and peer review. We maintain rigorous ethical protocols across all investigative activities conducted by Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM).

Our Mission

Paranormal Frame, a documentary initiative of Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM), is dedicated to the rigorous investigation and documentation of paranormal phenomena. We approach all investigations with scientific methodology, maintaining scholarly objectivity while acknowledging the complex nature of the unexplained.

Our work serves educational purposes and contributes to the broader understanding of phenomena that exist at the boundaries of conventional explanation. We maintain transparent reporting standards and are committed to presenting evidence and analysis in their proper context.

Our Philosophy

Core principles guiding our research

🔬

Scientific Rigor

All investigations employ validated methodology, calibrated instruments, and systematic data collection. We prioritize measurable evidence and reproducible observations.

🎯

Objective Analysis

We examine alternative hypotheses with equal consideration, acknowledge limitations of our methodology, and avoid confirmation bias in data interpretation.

📋

Transparent Documentation

Complete investigation methodologies, raw data, and reasoning are documented and available for peer review and critical examination.

💡

Intellectual Humility

We acknowledge the limits of current knowledge and remain open to evidence that challenges existing paradigms while maintaining critical thinking standards.

Research Standards

Our commitment to excellence

Methodology Documentation

Every investigation includes complete documentation of our procedures, equipment specifications, environmental baselines, and analysis protocols for full transparency and reproducibility.

Multi-Observer Protocols

Critical observations are verified by multiple independent observers to minimize subjective interpretation and increase reliability of reported phenomena.

Calibrated Instrumentation

All measurement devices undergo regular calibration and are selected based on proven reliability in scientific applications and paranormal research contexts.

Peer Review Process

Research findings undergo critical examination by qualified researchers before publication, ensuring quality standards and methodological integrity.

About Paranormal Frame - FAQs

Common questions about our organization and approach

Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM) is the parent organization behind Paranormal Frame. AERM focuses on systematic documentation and analysis of paranormal phenomena in India using scientific methodology. Paranormal Frame is our documentary and public research initiative dedicated to transparency and education in paranormal investigation.

Our team brings combined expertise across audio engineering, equipment calibration, historical research, environmental monitoring, and data analysis. Researchers participate in ongoing training in paranormal investigation protocols, equipment operation, and scientific methodology. While paranormal research exists at the boundaries of conventional academia, our team maintains standards of rigor, documentation, and critical thinking.

Paranormal Frame is funded through a combination of documentary initiatives, research grants, and community support. We maintain independence from external influence on research direction or findings interpretation. All investigation locations and case selection are based on research merit and accessibility, never on monetary considerations.

While peer review in paranormal research differs from conventional academic settings, we maintain rigorous standards of documentation, transparency, and critical analysis to ensure research integrity and quality.

We document and preserve evidence transparently, noting all alternative explanations explored during analysis. Unexplained phenomena are reported with full context about investigation conditions, equipment used, and hypotheses considered. We never assume supernatural causation without eliminating conventional explanations first.

All investigations at historically and spiritually significant locations are conducted with cultural respect and permission from relevant authorities. We acknowledge local traditions, consult historical records, and avoid sensationalism. Our research approach honors India's cultural heritage while maintaining scientific objectivity in evidence documentation and analysis.
