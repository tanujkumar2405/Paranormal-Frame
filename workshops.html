Paranormal Workshops

Professional Training & Education Programs

Comprehensive workshops designed to educate participants about evidence-based paranormal investigation, investigation ethics, critical thinking, and responsible inquiry practices in India.

About Our Workshops

AERM's workshop programs are designed for students, researchers, curious individuals, and professionals interested in paranormal investigation. Our workshops combine theoretical knowledge with practical training, emphasizing scientific methodology, critical thinking, and ethical investigation practices.

Each workshop is facilitated by experienced AERM investigators and researchers committed to providing evidence-based education and promoting responsible inquiry into unexplained phenomena.

Workshop Offerings

Professional training programs in paranormal investigation

📚

Introduction to Paranormal Investigation

Foundational workshop covering paranormal investigation basics, historical context, current research, methodology overview, and career pathways in paranormal research. Ideal for beginners interested in the field.

⚖️

Investigation Ethics

Comprehensive exploration of ethical principles in paranormal investigation including cultural sensitivity, participant rights, informed consent, data protection, and responsible reporting practices in India's cultural context.

📋

Evidence Collection & Documentation

Practical training in systematic evidence collection, documentation procedures, data preservation, chain-of-custody protocols, and maintaining investigative integrity throughout the investigation process.

🧪

Environmental Factors & Misidentification

Examination of environmental, geological, and architectural factors often misidentified as paranormal activity. Understanding natural phenomena, environmental influences, and scientific explanations for reported anomalies.

🧠

Human Perception & Psychology

Deep dive into psychology of perception, cognitive biases, suggestibility, memory reliability, and psychological factors influencing paranormal experiences and belief formation.

💭

Critical Thinking in Paranormal Research

Development of critical thinking skills applied to paranormal claims. Learning to evaluate evidence, identify logical fallacies, question assumptions, and maintain scientific objectivity in investigation.

🔍

Case Analysis Techniques

Systematic approach to analyzing paranormal cases including data evaluation, alternative hypothesis testing, pattern recognition, and drawing evidence-based conclusions from investigation data.

🛠️

Equipment Operation & Calibration

Hands-on training in operating and calibrating paranormal investigation equipment including EMF meters, thermal imaging cameras, audio recorders, and data logging devices. Understanding technical specifications and limitations.

Advanced Training Programs

Certified Paranormal Investigator Program

Comprehensive multi-module certification program covering all aspects of professional paranormal investigation. Participants complete theoretical coursework, practical training, and field investigations to earn AERM certification.

Field Investigation Training

Practical on-site training in actual investigation settings. Participants gain hands-on experience with equipment deployment, evidence collection, baseline measurement, and multi-observer protocols in real investigation environments.

Data Analysis & Research Methods

Advanced training in statistical analysis, research methodology, data interpretation, and scholarly approaches to paranormal phenomena. Includes techniques for converting investigation data into publishable research.

Investigator Leadership Program

Training for experienced investigators seeking leadership roles. Covers team management, investigation planning, protocol development, mentorship, and organizational skills for leading paranormal research teams.

Workshop FAQs
