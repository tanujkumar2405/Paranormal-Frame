Research Methodology Limitations

Paranormal Frame investigations employ validated scientific instrumentation and standardized protocols developed by AERM. However, paranormal research exists at the boundaries of conventional scientific understanding. All findings are subject to alternative hypothesis evaluation and remain open to reinterpretation as new information becomes available.

Data Interpretation

Measured anomalies do not constitute proof of paranormal activity. Electromagnetic field variations, thermal anomalies, audio phenomena, and other measured data may result from environmental factors, structural characteristics, mechanical systems, or other conventional causes. All alternative hypotheses must be thoroughly evaluated before alternative interpretations are considered.

Eyewitness Testimony

Documented eyewitness accounts are subject to human perception limitations, memory variation, and subjective interpretation. Eyewitness testimony alone does not constitute scientific evidence. Corroboration through instrumental measurement and multiple independent observers strengthens evidentiary assessment.

Environmental Variability

Measurement anomalies may correlate with:

  • Weather patterns and atmospheric conditions
  • Electrical and mechanical building systems
  • Geological and geomagnetic activity
  • Radio frequency interference
  • Seasonal environmental variation
  • Human activity and expectancy effects
