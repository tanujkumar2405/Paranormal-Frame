Privacy Policy

How Paranormal Frame and AERM handle personal data and privacy.

Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM) respects your privacy. This page describes how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through Paranormal Frame. We collect only the information necessary for research correspondence and case submissions. Personal data is stored securely and used only for the stated purposes. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Please contact us at anomalouseevidence@gmail.com with privacy inquiries.

Data Collected

We may collect contact information, location details, case descriptions, and media files submitted by users. Sensitive personal details should not be provided unless necessary for investigation and with explicit consent.

Use of Data

Data is used to coordinate investigations, communicate with contributors, and publish anonymised research reports. Identifying information is removed or redacted from public case studies unless explicit consent is provided.

Retention

We retain case-related data for research and archiving purposes. If you request removal of personal data, contact us and we will process the request in accordance with applicable law.

Information Sharing

We may share information with trusted collaborators and service providers when necessary to support investigations or case follow-up. Personal information is never sold, and identifying details are removed from public reports unless you explicitly consent to their use.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share. However, no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Avoid sharing highly sensitive personal data unless it is essential for an investigation.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at anomalouseevidence@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and retain information only as required for legal, reporting, or research obligations.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or your privacy rights, please contact us at anomalouseevidence@gmail.com.
