Research Articles & Publications

Detailed investigation findings and research documentation

Investigation Reports

Comprehensive reports documenting AERM investigations including methodology, equipment specifications, data collected, analysis performed, and findings. Each report maintains strict documentation standards for transparency and reproducibility.

Research Papers

Peer-reviewed research papers examining specific aspects of paranormal phenomena, investigation methodology, environmental analysis, or psychological factors. Papers maintain scientific rigor and submit to critical examination.

Case Studies

Detailed case studies of specific investigations providing historical context, location background, investigation procedures, results, and analysis. Case studies serve educational and research purposes for the broader paranormal research community.
