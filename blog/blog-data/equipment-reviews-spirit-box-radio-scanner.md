# Spirit Box Devices Under Review: What Can a Radio Scanner Actually Demonstrate?

Spirit-box devices rapidly scan radio frequencies or manipulate audio playback to create a stream of short fragments. Users may interpret fragments as answers. A technical review must begin with the device's actual operation: it is an audio source, not a validated instrument for detecting an entity.

**How scanning works.** A radio receiver tunes across frequencies. Depending on scan speed, bandwidth, squelch, modulation and reception conditions, it may produce brief portions of broadcast audio, noise or silence. A device may also use prerecorded banks or digitally generated sound. The exact design varies, so inspect the manual and do not assume every product uses the same mechanism.

**Review the hardware.** Document frequency coverage, tuning increments, scan speed, antenna design, output level, recording output, power supply and whether the device can be independently monitored. Test it in a known radio environment and record the local broadcast landscape. In India, available services and frequency allocations vary by region; verify current local spectrum information rather than assuming a channel is empty.

**Perceptual risks.** Human hearing is highly effective at recognising speech, even in incomplete signals. Once a listener expects a name or answer, ambiguous syllables may be interpreted accordingly. Replaying the same clip repeatedly can strengthen confidence without adding new information.

**A controlled listening study.** Record the full unedited session. Use time-stamped clips selected by a pre-defined rule, not only the apparent “hits.” Ask independent listeners who do not know the question or context to transcribe what they hear. Include blank or ordinary radio-control clips. Compare agreement rates and report disagreements.

**What the device can and cannot show.** It can demonstrate that a receiver produced audio under documented conditions. It cannot, by itself, establish who or what produced a fragment, whether it was responsive, or whether a voice was directed at an investigator.

**Conclusion.** Review spirit-box equipment as radio and audio technology. A responsible assessment focuses on repeatability, source transparency and blinded listening—not dramatic interpretations of isolated fragments.
