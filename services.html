Our Research & Field Services

Independent field research, rigorous documentation, and media production for the study of anomalous phenomena.

Paranormal Frame operates as the public research division of Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM), offering research services, field investigations, and documentary support designed to preserve and analyze anomalous evidence with scientific care.

Workshops, Courses & Training

Hands-on field workshops, short courses, and training sessions

Paranormal Frame offers practical learning opportunities for enthusiasts, students, and professionals. All training follows AERM's PFIRS standards and emphasises safe, ethical, and evidence-based practice.

Field Workshops

Hands-on evening and overnight workshops covering multi-sensor deployment, on-site documentation, and live evidence handling under supervision.

Short Courses

Modular short courses in EVP capture, EMF & RF surveying, thermal imaging, data logging, and basic signal analysis suitable for beginners and intermediates.

Professional Training

Bespoke training for organisations and research teams: equipment calibration, chain-of-custody procedures, and PFIRS-aligned case reporting.

Online Sessions & Webinars

Introductory theory and method webinars, plus recorded modules for remote learners and continuing professional development.

To book a workshop, request a customised course, or enquire about certification, contact us via the contact page. Group bookings and institutional training packages are available on request.

Learning Outcomes & Curriculum

  • Understand AERM's evidence-based investigation workflow and PFIRS documentation standards.
  • Learn how to operate EMF meters, thermal cameras, audio recorders, and environmental sensors in field conditions.
  • Build skills in case documentation, photography, sound analysis, and investigative note-taking.
  • Develop critical thinking for separating natural, architectural, and psychological explanations from anomalous observations.
  • Prepare for safe, ethical participation in field research and collaborative investigations.

Core modules include survey planning, equipment setup, environmental baseline measurement, evidence review, report generation, and ethical field behaviour.

How to engage with AERM services

A simple step-by-step process for booking and preparing for field research, training, or documentary support.

1. Choose the right service

Review our field investigation, workshop, and training offerings. Choose the package that best fits your research objective, site access, and team readiness.

2. Submit your request

Use the contact form to share location details, evidence background, desired dates, and any special requirements. Clear information helps us plan a safe, evidence-focused investigation.

3. Confirm and prepare

Confirm scheduling with the team, review checklist items for site access, and prepare any documents or permissions needed for fieldwork or workshops.

4. Participate and document

Attend the session with AERM investigators, follow research protocols, and collaborate with the team on documentation, evidence collection, and post-session reporting.

Services & Tours - Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about tours, booking, and participation

Click the Register button on the tour card, which will pre-select the tour in the contact form. Complete the contact form with your details and submit; we will review your request and confirm registration by email with payment instructions if required.
