Our Research & Field Services

Independent field research, rigorous documentation, and media production for the study of anomalous phenomena.

Paranormal Frame operates as the public research division of Anomalous Evidence Research & Media (AERM), offering research services, field investigations, and documentary support designed to preserve and analyze anomalous evidence with scientific care.

Services

Research, documentation, and media production aligned with our standards

🔎

Field Research & Site Documentation

On-site data collection, historical documentation, and calibrated environmental monitoring to create a complete record of anomalous occurrences.

🌡️

Environmental Anomaly Observation

Systematic measurement and monitoring of EMF, thermal, and acoustic variations to detect and triangulate environmental disturbances.

🧭

Myth vs Reality Investigations

Culturally informed investigations that test local narratives against empirical evidence to identify plausible explanations.

🧠

Cultural & Psychological Analysis

Contextual analysis of witness reports and cultural frameworks to understand perception, expectation, and sociocultural influences on reported phenomena.

🎥

Documentary & Digital Media Production

Professional production support for research dissemination: video, audio, and archival-quality documentation for future analysis and public education.

Services & Tours - Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about tours, booking, and participation

Click the Register button on the tour card, which will pre-select the tour in the contact form. Complete the contact form with your details and submit; we will review your request and confirm registration by email with payment instructions if required.

We will confirm your registration by email. A deposit or full payment may be required to secure your seat depending on the tour; payment methods and instructions are provided after we review your submission.

Typical inclusions: round-trip transport from the meeting point, guided access, basic investigation kit use, and on-site supervision. Accommodation and meals are usually not included unless explicitly stated.

Full refund if canceled 7+ days before the tour. Cancellations within 48 hours are eligible for a 50% refund. Exceptions may apply for emergencies or force majeure, which we assess case-by-case.

Many of our tours involve walking and uneven terrain. Minors are permitted but must be accompanied by a guardian; specific accessibility information is included in the tour details.

Select Request Custom Investigation in the tour select, or contact us via the form with details about location, dates, and objectives—we will respond with feasibility, pricing, and scheduling information.
