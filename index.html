Our Research Methodology

Scientific approach to investigating paranormal phenomena across Indian locations

01

Historical Documentation

Comprehensive research into location history, local legends, documented accounts, and cultural significance of Indian heritage sites. We establish baseline understanding of each location's supernatural narratives.

02

Environmental Assessment

Baseline measurements of electromagnetic fields, temperature, acoustic levels, and environmental conditions. We conduct measurements across different times and seasons to understand the site's natural parameters.

03

Multi-Phase Investigation

Structured investigation protocols utilizing calibrated instruments, multiple observers, and synchronized recording. We document phenomena across audio, visual, and electromagnetic spectrums during investigation phases.

04

Analysis & Publication

Systematic review and analysis of collected data with consideration of alternative hypotheses. Findings are compiled into detailed research reports documenting methodology, evidence, and conclusions for peer review and preservation.
