Our Team

Dedicated Professionals in Paranormal Research

Meet the experienced investigators, researchers, and field support professionals working together at AERM to advance evidence-based paranormal investigation in India.

Meet AERM

AERM (Anomalous Evidence Research & Media) is staffed by dedicated professionals who share commitment to scientific rigor, ethical investigation, and responsible documentation of paranormal phenomena. Our team brings diverse expertise in technology, research methodology, equipment calibration, field investigation, and public education.

Each team member undergoes rigorous training in paranormal investigation protocols, evidence preservation, equipment operation, and ethical investigation practices. Together, they work to maintain AERM's standards of credibility and scientific excellence.
